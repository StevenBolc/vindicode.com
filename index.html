Why I do this

The subscription model is good for software companies and bad for everyone else. Your tools stop working if you stop paying. Your data is stuck in someone else's cloud. The moment they decide to pivot, raise prices, or shut down — you're the one who loses.

I got tired of watching that happen to people. So I build differently. Every app I ship runs on your machine, stores your data locally, and costs a flat price. You own it the same way you own any other tool you buy.

— Steven, Vindicode
